Video Project Link "Millersville's Sports Facilities"

The purpose of the video project was to create a video production the promotes one of Millersville's campus features. We decided to focus on Millersville's athletic facilities.

For the project first we had to pick a topic and create a story board and script of the locations and what we were going to say. Then we captured the video on the Sony Handie Cam provided. Lastly, we edited our video and presented it to the class.
