Bookmark Designs

For our Bookmark Project our main goal what to create an inspiring bookmark for elementary level students at Eshleman Elementary School.

We started this project by drawing 4 bookmarks on paper then we picked our 2 favorite designs and designed then in Adobe InDesign on the computers. After that we picked our favorite desing and then go into groups and then we made our printing plate on the Presstek Platemaker lastly, we printed the bookmarks out on the Ryobi Printing Press.
